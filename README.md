One line in the Moon's own voice, answering at last the wishes, the howls
and the late-night promises of everyone staring up at it — the entry for
make.fun's The Moon Finally Answers Back, where a judge model compares
entries two at a time and the funniest moon comebacks take the $2 prize
(1st $0.70, 2nd $0.50, 3rd $0.35, 4th $0.25, 5th $0.20).

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 3 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever asked how I'm doing.

The line was chosen by a local pairwise round-robin tournament over 18 fresh
candidate lines, scored on the judge brief's five criteria in order: speaks
as the Moon answering wishes, howls and late-night promises; funny on one
read; wry, old, a little tired Moon voice; lands as a comeback; wastes no
characters. It won 17 of 17 pairings. The tournament script and full table
live in the build directory, outside this repo.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line (107 characters, no trailing newline) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Stdlib only,
deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
